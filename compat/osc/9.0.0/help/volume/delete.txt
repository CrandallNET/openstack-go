usage: openstack volume delete [-h] [--force | --cascade] [--remote]
                               <volume> [<volume> ...]

Delete volume(s)

positional arguments:
  <volume>    Volume(s) to delete (name or ID)

options:
  -h, --help  show this help message and exit
  --force     Attempt forced removal of volume(s), regardless of state
              (defaults to False)
  --cascade   Remove any snapshots along with volume(s) (defaults to False)
  --remote    Specify this parameter to unmanage a volume.
